Toneo

📍 Los Angeles, CA · Nov 2026 – Present

A team project: an iOS AI keyboard that helps users reply in English. I lead the project and own the product, all of the AI and the backend. We expect to launch on the App Store in December and run a first small-group user test.

Onboarding

The keyboard

Tone-personalization agent

Built a scenario example library (daily, professional, casual, group work) from LLM-generated replies that we annotated and edited by hand, then combined it with phrases extracted from the user’s WeChat screenshots for few-shot retrieval, so English replies keep the user’s voice. In internal team testing, the few-shot version was preferred in 90% of comparisons.

AI memory

Extracts the user’s most frequent phrases and personal information from their chat screenshots. Every fact must quote the original message and is checked by code, so the model can’t make things up. Raw chats stay on the phone.

Model selection

Ran a user blind evaluation of different models under three conditions (direct generation, known recipient and channel, and a tone profile), and chose Gemini Flash-Lite as the default.

Backend service

Built the backend in Python FastAPI on Vercel, with three core endpoints: streaming reply generation, chat-screenshot summary and user-memory analysis. The generation pipeline covers scenario and tone-level detection, vector retrieval of examples (Gemini Embedding ranking, MMR de-duplication, a similarity floor), multi-turn edits and output validation. The similarity floor was calibrated by hand scoring, cutting irrelevant examples from 41% to 25%.
