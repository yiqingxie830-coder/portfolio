BACKGROUND

GROWTH

Design school trained my intuition. AI gave me a second, equally exciting way to think. Machine learning and data pushed me from instinct toward analysis: run the experiment, test the idea, find the pattern. What draws me most is how fast AI is lowering the bar to making things, so ideas that used to stay imagined can now be built, tested and iterated on quickly. Stepping into AI felt like opening a Skinner box and walking into a world built on math and logic. What I know keeps getting built up and torn down here, and that keeps me learning and stretching what I understand.
